01

A year in a notebook

Quatréa began as a single journal kept by our founding perfumer: one page for every day of the year, each recording the smell of the air. After twelve months, four voices emerged — four seasons, four characters.

Quatre + ére

Our name joins the French quatre — four — with ère, an era. Four eras of the year, each lasting just long enough to be missed. The quartered circle in our emblem is the year itself.
